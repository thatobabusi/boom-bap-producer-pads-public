# SEQ tab: step sequencer & Roll

**In one line:** program a beat, one pad at a time, on a 16-step grid.

## What this part of the plugin is for

A step sequencer is the classic drum-machine way of making a pattern: one
bar is split into 16 boxes (steps), and you switch on the steps where a pad
should play. Each pad has its own pattern, so you build a beat by picking the
kick and lighting its steps, then the snare, and so on. The sequencer plays
in time with your DAW. There are also tools that write patterns for you
(genre templates, randomize, humanize), and **Roll**, which repeats a pad
rapidly while you hold it.

## Questions this answers

- *How do I make a beat quickly?* Use **Generate...**, pick a genre, assign your kick/snare/hat pads, and Generate.
- *My beat sounds robotic.* Use Swing and Humanize.
- *How do I stop Randomize from changing my kick?* Right-click the kick pad and mark it Favorite.
- *Why doesn't it play?* The sequencer follows your DAW's transport: press play in the DAW. Standalone, use **Seq Play** (see [Saving & standalone](../../saving-and-standalone.md)).
